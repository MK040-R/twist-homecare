We did the homework on laundry, so you don't have to.

People spend ₹2,000 on a dress and check the fabric, the fit and the reviews, then wash it in whatever detergent happens to be at home. Twist exists to close that gap, with laundry care from people who have done the thinking for you.

What laundry ads taught us, and what's actually true.

What we were toldMore foam means a better clean.
What's trueFoam is a side effect of some cleaning agents. It says nothing about how clean your clothes get.
What we were toldBrighter whites are cleaner whites.
What's trueOptical brighteners are dyes that make fabric reflect blue light. Clothes look brighter without being any cleaner.
What we were toldA longer wash is a better wash.
What's trueClothes that are more sweaty than dirty come clean in a short cycle, if the detergent is made for it.
What we were toldIf it smells strong, it's clean.
What's trueFragrance makes laundry pleasant. It doesn't remove dirt, and it can cover a smell that's still there.
What we were toldNatural is always better.
What's trueA short, natural-sounding ingredient list doesn't make a better formula. What each ingredient does in the wash does.
What we were toldOne detergent works for everything.
What's trueTowels, gym wear and underwear get dirty in different ways, so they need different chemistry.
