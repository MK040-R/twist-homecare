What most detergents get wrong.

Why we're making our own.

Pick a problem to see why it happens and the evidence behind it.

01

Too harsh

Detergents are often made strongly alkaline, because a high pH breaks down grease and stains fast. The same alkalinity can leave skin dry and irritated, and wears fibres down wash after wash.

Proof

Laundry powders typically wash at pH 10 or higher. Healthy skin sits below pH 5.

Skin pH: Lambers et al., Int. J. Cosmetic Science, 2006
What we're doing

Each formula is being built to clean at the gentlest pH that does the job. We will print the number on the pack.
