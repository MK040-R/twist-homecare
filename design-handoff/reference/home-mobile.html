What most detergents get wrong.

Why we're making our own.

Tap a problem to see why it happens.

Detergents are often made strongly alkaline because a high pH breaks down grease fast. The same alkalinity can dry out skin and wear fibres down.

Proof

Laundry powders typically wash at pH 10 or higher. Healthy skin sits below pH 5.

Skin pH: Lambers et al., 2006
What we're doing

Each formula is being built to clean at the gentlest pH that does the job.
